Conflicts (both passages, my recommendation)

C1

"This gets sharper as you log."

Your item 7: label "First estimate" … and the line "This gets sharper as you log."CLAUDE.md, Model facts: "Never write 'gets sharper as you log'."

Recommend: drop the line. Your second message already says what replaces it (the log-count label), and the model's range does not shrink with logs today.

C2

Morning rating

Your item 13: spec R2, 0–10, 11 tap targets in two rows, no default.DECISIONS.md, D5 (2 Oct): "a 1-10 slider as drawn in Figma, the thumb resting on 3"; 11B "use the new bar to all bars".

Recommend: follow item 13 (latest instruction) and record that it reverses D5. Stored value becomes 0–10; the ×10 for the 0–100 line happens only where it is drawn.

C3

Selected state colour

Your item 12: selected Allow = raised #1E1E1E + 1.5 pt #F5F5F7 stroke + check, not sage; sage only on Continue.CLAUDE.md colour table: state.selected = sage. DECISIONS D5: selected pill = sage fill, no check (Figma). Size tokens have 1 and 2 pt strokes, no 1.5.

Recommend: change state.selected to the new style everywhere (consent, privacy, evening log, language, first log), so sage stays the one action on each screen; add a 1.5 pt stroke token. Q2.

C4

A sheet with no way out but a swipe

Your item 9: no "Not now"; swipe-down dismisses.CLAUDE.md: every interactive element has accessible states; WCAG 2.1.1 / 2.5.1: a path that needs a gesture needs an alternative.

Recommend: keep one button, add a small "Close" icon (not a second button) so VoiceOver and Switch Control users can leave. Equal weight does not apply: this is not health-data consent; the system alert is the consent.

C5

"Back up your history"

Your item 10: "Back up your history" (create account).GAP G56 and the auth demo note: accounts live on this phone until the app closes; nothing is synced.

Recommend: "Keep your history with an account" plus the demo note, until sync exists. Q3.

C6

Welcome's one action

Your item 5: guest is the default; account is optional.Welcome today: "Create account" is the liquid metal primary (your 4B); guest is a text link.

Recommend: liquid metal "Get started" (guest), "I already have an account" as text; Create account comes after the first evening log. Q1.

C7

Undoing 8B

Your item 14: remove the stat-card grid and the hatch.DECISIONS D5: 8B chosen ("let's try"); the hatch fix shipped this morning.

Recommend: follow item 14; keep the 9B day list. Record as "8B tried and removed".

C8

Hidden profile fields vs 11B and 12B

Your item 16: show Sessions only; hide days, time, work days.DECISIONS D5: 11B slider and 12B time picker live in Edit profile; item 13 moves the rating off the slider.

Recommend: hide them in Profile and Edit profile; keep both components in the gallery for when the engine reads the fields.

C9

"8 of 10" and the fixtures

Your item 15: show felt as "8 of 10".fixtures/marta-week.json: felt ratings are on 0–100 (65, 60, 50, 45, 60, 55), not whole tenths.

Recommend: one decimal for fixture days ("6.5 of 10"), whole numbers for new ratings; or Person B rounds the fixtures. Q4.

C10

First launch vs Marta's scripted history

Your items 6–7: day 1, First log, "First estimate".Demo build: fixtures give Marta six logged days before 5 Oct (Progress shows 5 of 6).

Q5: in the demo build, does first launch start from an empty history (scores then need the GAP engine work), or keep Marta's history (then "First estimate" never shows)?

C11

"Max 2 lines"

Your item 8: one block, max 2 lines.CLAUDE.md: Dynamic Type stays on; text wraps, never truncates.

Recommend: 2 lines at the default text size in English and Polish (a copy rule, tested at 390 pt); larger text wraps freely.

C12

"So a wider range"

Your second message: "Fewer logs this week, so a wider range"; the range must widen when logs are sparse.CLAUDE.md model facts: the range is ±17.2, not calibrated; "Do not add model facts."

Recommend: show "Fewer logs this week" alone until Person B widens the range; then add the clause. GAP.
