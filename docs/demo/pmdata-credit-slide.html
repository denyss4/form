Built on PMData

Form's model is trained on PMData, a public dataset from Simula: 16 recreational athletes in Norway, who logged readiness, sleep, mood, stress, training and more over about five months.

Licence. CC BY 4.0. We built a daily table from the raw data and trained a ridge regression on it.

Cite. Thambawita, V., Hicks, S. A., et al. (2020). PMData: a sports logging dataset. Proceedings of the 11th ACM Multimedia Systems Conference (MMSys '20), 231–236. doi.org/10.1145/3339825.3394926
